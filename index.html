Design

Three passes. No shortcuts.

The order matters — each stage depends on the one before it.

  1. 01

    Milled

    A single titanium billet is cut on a five-axis CNC over 40 minutes, then hand-polished to a satin finish.

  2. 02

    Tuned

    Acoustic engineers voice every driver by ear against a reference room before any DSP is applied.

  3. 03

    Calibrated

    An in-app ear scan builds a personal response curve, matched to the shape of your own ear canal.

Finish

Choose your metal.

Three finishes, one shell. Anodized, not painted — the color goes all the way through.
